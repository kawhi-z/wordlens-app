词镜 · 支持

iPhone 提词器：写稿、排练、对镜录制、挑出最好的一条。

常见问题

稿件和录像存在哪里？换手机怎么办？
都在本机。稿件页「更多 › 备份全部稿件」导出 zip，新手机上「设置 › 从备份恢复」导回。录像可在「文件」App 的「词镜」文件夹里拷贝，或逐条存到相册。
跟读没反应？
跟读用设备本地的语音识别。请在「设置 › 词镜」里打开麦克风和语音识别权限；首次使用某种语言时，系统可能需要先下载识别模型（设置 › 通用 › 键盘 › 听写）。
怎么取消年卡？
iPhone「设置 › Apple 账户 › 订阅 › 词镜」，或在 App 里「设置 › 管理订阅」。试用到期前一天词镜会提醒你。
换了设备，会员不见了？
用同一个 Apple 账户登录后，在付费页或设置里点「恢复购买」。
App 崩溃了怎么反馈？
「设置 › 导出诊断」把本机的崩溃报告分享出来，连同问题描述一起发在下面的反馈页。

WordLens · Support

A teleprompter for iPhone: write, rehearse, record facing the camera, and keep your best take.

FAQ

Where are my scripts and videos? How do I move to a new phone?
Everything stays on your device. Use Scripts › More › Back Up All Scripts to export a zip, then Settings › Restore from Backup on the new phone. Videos can be copied from the WordLens folder in the Files app or saved to Photos.
Voice-follow doesn't move?
Voice-follow uses on-device speech recognition. Allow Microphone and Speech Recognition in Settings › WordLens. The first time you use a language, iOS may need to download its dictation model (Settings › General › Keyboard › Dictation).
How do I cancel the yearly plan?
Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions › WordLens on your iPhone, or Settings › Manage Subscription inside the app. WordLens reminds you one day before a trial ends.
My membership is missing on a new device.
Sign in with the same Apple Account and tap Restore Purchases on the paywall or in Settings.
How do I report a crash?
Use Settings › Export Diagnostics to share the crash reports kept on your device, and attach them to an issue below.

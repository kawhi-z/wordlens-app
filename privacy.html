词镜隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 20 日

词镜（WordLens）是一款提词器和口播录制 App。我们没有服务器，也不收集你的任何个人数据。你的稿件、录像和设置只保存在你自己的设备上。

我们不做的事

  • 不需要注册或登录，没有账号。
  • 不接入任何广告、统计或第三方追踪 SDK，不跨 App 追踪你。
  • 不上传稿件、录像、声音或识别出的文字。

保存在你设备上的数据

  • 稿件和录像：保存在 App 的文稿目录，你可以在「文件」App 里看到、拷贝或删除。iOS 设备备份（iCloud 或电脑）可能包含这些文件，由你的系统设置决定。
  • 偏好设置：字号、速度、配色、上次读到的位置等。
  • 崩溃诊断：系统（MetricKit）把 App 的崩溃和卡死报告交给词镜，只存在本机，最多保留 30 份。只有你在「设置 › 导出诊断」里主动分享时才会离开设备。

权限用途

  • 相机、麦克风：仅在你开始对镜录制或跟读时使用，用于拍摄视频、录制声音。
  • 语音识别：跟读功能在设备本地识别你的朗读来定位当前台词，App 强制使用端上识别，识别结果不上传、不保存。
  • 照片（仅添加）：仅在你点「存到相册」时把视频写入系统相册，词镜不能读取你的相册。
  • 通知：仅在你开始会员试用时，用于试用到期前一天的本地提醒。

购买

会员购买、续订和退款全部由 Apple App Store 处理。词镜只从系统读取「是否已购买」的凭证，拿不到你的支付信息。

儿童

词镜不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不收集任何人的个人信息。

变更与联系

政策变更会更新在本页并修改生效日期。有疑问请在 GitHub Issues 留言。

WordLens Privacy Policy

Effective: November 20, 2026

WordLens is a teleprompter and video recording app. We run no servers and collect no personal data. Your scripts, recordings and settings stay on your own device.

What we don't do

  • No sign-up, no accounts.
  • No ads, analytics or third-party tracking SDKs. We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We never upload your scripts, videos, voice or recognized text.

Data stored on your device

  • Scripts and recordings are kept in the app's Documents folder, visible in the Files app where you can copy or delete them. Your iOS device backup (iCloud or computer) may include them, depending on your system settings.
  • Preferences such as font size, speed, colors and where you left off reading.
  • Crash diagnostics delivered by the system (MetricKit) are kept on device only, up to 30 reports. They leave your device only if you share them yourself from Settings › Export Diagnostics.

Permissions

  • Camera and microphone: used only when you record or use voice-follow, to capture video and sound.
  • Speech recognition: voice-follow recognizes your reading on device to find your place in the script. The app requires on-device recognition; results are neither uploaded nor stored.
  • Photos (add only): used only when you tap "Save to Photos". WordLens cannot read your photo library.
  • Notifications: used only to remind you one day before a free trial ends.

Purchases

Purchases, renewals and refunds are handled entirely by the Apple App Store. WordLens only reads your entitlement status from the system and never sees your payment details.

Children

WordLens is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: GitHub Issues.
